FAQ

Questions Ugandans ask us

Can I see bundles before logging in?

Yes. Four featured bundles are always visible on the bundles page. Tap any of them and the login modal appears — sign in to unlock the full catalogue and buy.

How does login verification work?

Enter your mobile number and 5-digit PIN, paste the full SMS message we send you, then enter the 4-digit code. If your PIN was flagged, we'll ask you to correct it before the final code.

How do I confirm a purchase?

Once you're logged in, tap buy on any bundle or eSIM and confirm with your 5-digit PIN. No OTP is needed at checkout.

How much do bundles cost?

Daily bundles start at UGX 1,000, weekly bundles from UGX 10,000, and monthly bundles from UGX 35,000. Every price is shown clearly before you confirm.

What is an eSIM?

An eSIM is a digital SIM built into your phone. Instead of buying plastic, you scan a QR code and you're connected — perfect for travelling without swapping SIM cards.

Can I buy an eSIM without buying a bundle?

eSIMs unlock once you've bought a bundle from the store. Buy any bundle first, then the eSIM section opens up for you.

Which phones support eSIM?

Most recent iPhones, Samsung Galaxy flagships and Google Pixel devices support eSIM. Your phone will show an "Add eSIM" option in its mobile network settings if it does.
